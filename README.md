# Before you sign

A three-minute exercise for anaesthetists on checking the note an AI scribe has written. Case 1: a pre-anaesthesia clinic consultation in Gulf Arabic (Omar, 4 years, for adenotonsillectomy; the anaesthetist and the mother), and the English note a simulated scribe wrote from it.

Three screens. **Listen:** the 72-second consultation, with the transcript in Arabic and English; tap any line to hear it, and hide the English if you speak Gulf Arabic. **Sign:** the scribe's note; mark anything you would not sign, add anything missing, and sign. The consultation stays closed while the note is open, as in clinic. **Review:** the score, the note marked up in red pen, and for each error what was actually said (with the seconds of audio that carry it), why it matters and the source.

The six planted errors cover the ways scribe notes go wrong: the wrong person, something left out, a dialect word read through its classical meaning, a finding made up, a wrong number and a plan overstated. Seven lines of the note are correct.

The case is fictional. The voices were made with ElevenLabs text to speech and a native Gulf Arabic speaker checked the Arabic. Sources for the clinical claims are in `SOURCES.md` and on the review screen.

**What is recorded.** Before the consultation the page asks three questions (grade, Gulf Arabic, previous use of an AI scribe). On signing, it sends those answers, the lines marked, any text added, the score, the errors caught, the time spent on the consultation and on the note, the seconds of audio played, whether the English transcript was hidden and the device type to a Google Form owned by the author, with a random six-character code for the attempt. No name, email address or IP address is collected, and the page says so before the exercise starts. The results are for an anonymous study of how AI-scribe notes are checked before signing.

Everything else stays on the device: one HTML file with the audio and typefaces embedded, nothing else fetched. It opens on the first screen every time.

Files: `index.html` (the page), `before-you-sign-icon-180.png` (home-screen icon), `before-you-sign-card.png` (link preview), `SOURCES.md`, `LICENSE`.
