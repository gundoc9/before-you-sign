# Sources

How each source was checked for case 1, with a link (7 October 2026). Every clinical claim on the review screen is attributed to one of these.

- **von Ungern-Sternberg and colleagues** - von Ungern-Sternberg BS, Boda K, Chambers NA, et al. Risk assessment for respiratory complications in paediatric anaesthesia: a prospective cohort study. Lancet 2010;376:773-83.  
  Abstract and results read: 9,297 questionnaires; upper respiratory tract infection with symptoms (RR 2.05) or within 2 weeks (RR 2.34) raised the risk of perioperative respiratory adverse events; a positive respiratory history (nocturnal dry cough, wheeze, eczema) raised the risk of bronchospasm (RR 8.46) and laryngospasm (RR 4.13). Errors: Wrong person, Left out. [Open the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)61193-2)
- **Eberhart and colleagues** - Eberhart LHJ, Geldner G, Kranke P, et al. The development and validation of a risk score to predict the probability of postoperative vomiting in pediatric patients. Anesth Analg 2004;99:1630-7.  
  Abstract read: four factors (surgery 30 minutes or longer, age 3 years or older, strabismus surgery, a history of postoperative vomiting in the child or of postoperative vomiting or nausea and vomiting in a parent or sibling); vomiting in 9%, 10%, 30%, 55% and 70% of children with 0 to 4 factors. Error: Dialect. [Open the paper](https://doi.org/10.1213/01.ANE.0000135639.57715.6C)
- **Thomas and colleagues** - Thomas M, Morrison C, Newton R, Schindler E. Consensus statement on clear fluids fasting for elective pediatric general anesthesia. Paediatr Anaesth 2018;28:411-4.  
  Abstract read: clear fluids until 1 hour before elective general anaesthesia (APAGBI, ESPA and ADARPEF). Error: Wrong number. [Open the paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/pan.13370)
- **Frykholm and colleagues** - Frykholm P, Disma N, Andersson H, et al. Pre-operative fasting in children: a guideline from the European Society of Anaesthesiology and Intensive Care. Eur J Anaesthesiol 2022;39:4-25.  
  Abstract read: clear fluid fasting reduced to 1 hour (GRADE 1C or 1B). Error: Wrong number. [Open the paper](https://doi.org/10.1097/EJA.0000000000001599)
- **Taylor and colleagues** - Taylor SL, Jost M, MacDonald S, et al. Quality of clinical notes created by ambient listening generative AI: pragmatic prospective pilot study. JMIR Med Inform 2026;14:e86474.  
  Abstract read: 356 AI-drafted notes reviewed at one US health system; accidental omissions in 18%, hallucinations in 11.5%, accidental inclusions in 9.3%; 5.3% carried an error rated as a serious risk. Errors: Left out, Made up. [Open the paper](https://doi.org/10.2196/86474)
- **The root طرش** - Lane EW, An Arabic-English Lexicon (1863-93), and Dozy R, Supplement aux dictionnaires arabes (1881), as reproduced on arabiclexicon.hawramani.com.  
  Lane and the classical lexicons give deafness (الطرش) and deaf (أطرش); Dozy records the colloquial sense vomited. Error: Dialect. [Open the entry](https://arabiclexicon.hawramani.com/%d8%b7%d8%b1%d8%b4/)
